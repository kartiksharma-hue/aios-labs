Answers

Google Ads Agency in Lucknow: frequently asked

Do Hindi keywords work in Google Ads?

Transliterated ones do, typed in English script, and they are common across local categories in this market. They usually carry lower competition with comparable intent, which lowers blended cost per conversion when they are added properly.

Our conversions happen on the phone. Can that be tracked?

Yes, with call tracking numbers and call conversions imported into the account. Without it the algorithm optimises toward form submissions, which in phone-led categories is optimising for the wrong outcome entirely.

How should an education business budget through the year?

Front-loaded around intake windows, not evenly. Demand in this category compresses into a few weeks, and campaigns launched inside the window spend the peak in the learning phase rather than converting.

Are Google Ads cheaper in Lucknow than in Delhi?

Generally yes, often substantially, because advertiser density is lower. Average order values are also lower though, so the cost advantage does not automatically translate into easier profitability.
