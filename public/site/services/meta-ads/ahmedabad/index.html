Meta Ads Agency in Ahmedabad: frequently asked

Is click-to-WhatsApp better than sending traffic to our website?

In this market, usually yes, because it matches how the sale actually completes. The caveat is that it only works if enquiries are answered quickly. A WhatsApp campaign with slow replies performs worse than a website funnel, not better.

How fast do we need to respond to enquiries?

Within minutes for the best results. Connect rates fall sharply after the first fifteen, and continue falling through the first hour. Automated acknowledgement plus a real reply quickly afterwards is the practical standard.

Can Meta work for wholesale and B2B buyers?

Yes, though with different creative and qualification. Capability and catalogue demonstration with qualifying questions in the form or the first WhatsApp message filters out retail enquiries that would otherwise flood the pipeline.

What does this cost to run properly?

Around ₹1L a month in media is a workable starting point for a local consumer category, less for a narrowly targeted wholesale campaign. The bigger determinant of results here is response handling, which costs time rather than media budget.
