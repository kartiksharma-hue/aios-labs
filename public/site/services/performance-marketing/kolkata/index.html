Kolkata offers the cheapest reach of any Indian metro and the least forgiving price sensitivity. Both facts have to shape the account, or the low costs become a trap rather than an advantage.

Impressions here are inexpensive enough that almost any campaign looks efficient at the top of the funnel. The difficulty appears further down: average order values run lower than in western metros, discount expectation is higher, and a campaign optimised on cost per click can generate volume that never reaches contribution break-even.

So Kolkata accounts should be built and judged on contribution margin from the start, not on cost per click or even ROAS in isolation. The other structural factor is the festive calendar. Durga Puja and the surrounding weeks concentrate an outsized share of annual consumer demand into a short window, and a budget spread evenly across twelve months systematically underinvests in the only period that matters most.

Performance Marketing Agency in Kolkata: frequently asked

Our Kolkata costs look great but we are not making money. Why?

Cheap impressions and clicks in this market can produce healthy-looking top-of-funnel metrics while average order value and margin sit below what the campaign needs. Setting break-even ROAS from contribution margin, and judging the account against it, usually exposes the gap immediately.

How much does the festive season matter in Kolkata?

More than in any other Indian metro. Demand concentrates heavily around Durga Puja and the following weeks, and both budget and creative should be planned backwards from that window. Spending evenly across the year forfeits most of the available return.

Does Bengali creative make a difference?

In mass-market and local categories, consistently. It usually lowers cost per result and improves engagement quality, and it is missing from the large majority of accounts we take over. For premium and B2B categories the effect is smaller.

What budget works for Kolkata?

Media costs are lower here, so ₹80,000 to ₹1L a month can be workable for a competitive consumer category, which is less than most metros. The constraint is usually margin rather than reach, so the offer economics matter more than the budget size.
