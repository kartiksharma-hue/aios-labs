Performance Marketing Agency in Noida: frequently asked

Are you actually based in Noida?

Yes. AIOS Labs operates from Noida, Uttar Pradesh. It is the only city where we claim a physical presence, and we deliberately do not claim local addresses in other cities we serve.

Can you visit our Noida office?

Yes, and for local clients we usually do at the start of an engagement. Understanding the product, the operations and the margin structure in person consistently produces better work than a call does.

Do you work with Greater Noida and Noida Extension businesses?

Yes: the fulfilment and warehousing corridor there has a growing D2C base, and it is a short trip. Same for Noida Extension and the Expressway sectors.

What is the minimum budget to work together?

For paid media we generally want to see at least ₹1.5L a month in spend for the account to produce usable data. Smaller local businesses are often better served by a local SEO and website engagement first, which we will say if that is the case.
