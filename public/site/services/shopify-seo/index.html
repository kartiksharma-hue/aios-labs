Shopify is excellent at selling and careless about search. It ships URL patterns that produce the same product at several addresses, collection pages with a heading and a grid and nothing else, and a filtering system that can generate thousands of crawlable combinations of the same twenty items. None of that is a mistake you made; it is what the platform does by default.

That is why generic SEO advice underperforms on Shopify. Fixing a store means knowing where the platform hides its canonicals, which app scripts are loading on every page for a feature used on one, and what a Liquid theme is doing to Core Web Vitals. Scope

What shopify seo includes

URL and canonical audit

Shopify serves the same product under /products/ and under every /collections/*/products/ path it belongs to. We map what is being duplicated, confirm the canonicals actually resolve, and stop the crawler spending its budget on copies.

Collection page depth

A collection page with a title and a product grid is a thin page competing for your most commercial keyword. We build the buying guidance, comparisons and FAQs that give those pages something to rank with.

Faceted navigation control

Filter and sort parameters can generate thousands of crawlable URLs that all show the same items in a different order. We decide which combinations deserve indexing and stop the rest from consuming crawl budget.

App and theme performance

Most Shopify speed problems are app scripts loading site-wide for a feature on one page, plus a theme doing more work than it needs to. We identify what to remove, defer or replace, and measure the result rather than assuming it.

Product and review schema

Product, offer, breadcrumb and review markup implemented correctly, including how variants are represented, so listings can earn rich results instead of being ignored for malformed data.

Content that matches how people buy

Comparison, material, sizing, care and pricing questions all carry commercial intent and almost no competition in most niches. Those pages capture buyers weeks before a paid ad would reach them.

Answers

Shopify SEO: frequently asked

Why is my Shopify store not ranking?

In most stores we audit it is a combination of three things: the same product reachable at several URLs so authority is split, collection pages too thin to rank for the terms they target, and a theme slowed by apps loading site-wide. All three are platform defaults rather than mistakes, and all three are fixable without leaving Shopify.

Does Shopify handle SEO automatically?

It handles the basics: sitemaps, canonical tags on products and reasonably clean URLs. What it does not handle is thin collection pages, filter URLs generating near-duplicate combinations, app scripts slowing every page, or content that answers what buyers actually search. Those are where the work is.

What are duplicate product URLs on Shopify?

Shopify serves a product at /products/handle and also at /collections/collection-name/products/handle for every collection it belongs to. Canonicals usually point back correctly, but internal links, sitemaps and third-party apps often reference the collection version, which splits signals and wastes crawl budget on a large catalogue.

Will removing apps break my store?

We do not remove anything without checking what depends on it. The usual finding is that several apps load their scripts on every page for a feature used on one, which can be limited rather than removed. Where an app genuinely earns its weight, it stays.

How long does Shopify SEO take?

Technical fixes can show in Search Console within weeks, because they change what gets crawled and indexed rather than how you compete. Collection and content rankings realistically take four to eight months. Anyone promising commercial rankings in a month is describing a keyword nobody searches.

Do you work on Shopify Plus?

Yes. Plus adds capability rather than changing the fundamentals: the same URL structure, the same collection and faceting questions, the same theme performance considerations, usually at a catalogue size where crawl budget matters more.

Can you help if we are migrating to Shopify?

Yes, and that is the point to involve someone. Most traffic lost in a migration is lost to unmapped redirects and a new URL structure nobody planned. Both are straightforward before launch and expensive afterwards.
