Methodology

How an engagement actually runs

1. Diagnose before proposing

The first deliverable is an audit, not a plan. Accounts, analytics, site, offer and margin get examined before anyone recommends a budget. Most engagements find the constraint somewhere other than where the client expected.

2. Fix the measurement first

Nothing scales on top of broken data. Conversion definitions, server-side events, deduplication and a reconciliation against actual revenue come before optimisation, because every decision afterwards depends on them.

3. Test for signal, not for records

The first six weeks are about learning which creative, audience, page and offer combinations work. Chasing a record month in week two produces a spike and no repeatable system.

4. Scale what is proven

Budget moves behind combinations that have demonstrated they work, in increments the account and the operations side can absorb without breaking.

5. Compound

Retention, retargeting and basket value get layered on so growth stops depending entirely on the cost of acquiring the next new customer.

About AIOS Labs: frequently asked

What is AIOS Labs?

AIOS Labs is a performance marketing and creative agency based in Noida, Uttar Pradesh, India. It is run by Kartik Sharma, a performance marketer, and Kartik Sharma, a social media creative strategist. The agency works with D2C, ecommerce and growth-focused businesses on paid media, SEO, conversion optimisation, creative production and marketing automation.

Who runs AIOS Labs?

Kartik Sharma leads performance marketing and has managed over ₹50 crore in ad spend across D2C, real estate, healthcare, education and B2B. Kartik Sharma leads social media creative strategy, with agency experience across B2C, D2C, fashion, retail, travel, cleantech and NGO brands.

Where is AIOS Labs located?

Noida, Uttar Pradesh, India. That is the only physical location. The agency works with clients across Delhi NCR and the rest of India remotely, and with brands in the United States, the United Arab Emirates, Australia and Canada.

What industries does AIOS Labs work with?

D2C and ecommerce, fashion and apparel, beauty and personal care, health and nutrition, real estate, healthcare and clinics, edtech and coaching, B2B and SaaS, travel and hospitality, and local service businesses such as salons and gyms.

Does AIOS Labs guarantee results?

No. ROAS depends on margin, offer, price point, market and repeat rate, most of which sit outside an ad account. The commitment is to a documented process, a testing cadence, transparent reporting reconciled against real revenue, and client ownership of all accounts and data.

How does AIOS Labs charge?

Monthly retainers for ongoing media, SEO and content work, and fixed project fees for audits, tracking implementations and website or landing page builds. Pricing is quoted after seeing the account, because the amount of work varies enormously by situation. Ad spend is always a separate line from the fee.
