Mumbai holds two economies that rarely overlap elsewhere. There is the media, entertainment, fashion and consumer-brand cluster running from Andheri and Lower Parel, and there is the financial services core around BKC, Nariman Point and Fort. The first buys creative; the second buys measurement.

Because Mumbai is where most of India's content production capacity sits, brands here tend to have better creative assets than brands elsewhere, and worse funnel discipline. The gap we most often find in Mumbai accounts is not the ad, it is what happens after the click.

What the local economy runs on

Financial services and capital markets, media and entertainment production, fashion and apparel, the country's densest D2C and consumer brand base, real estate, hospitality and a dense professional services layer.

Answers

Mumbai: frequently asked

Do you work with Mumbai brands remotely?

Yes. We are based in Noida and run Mumbai accounts over video and shared dashboards, which is how the majority of performance marketing engagements operate regardless of city. Shoots and on-ground production in Mumbai are coordinated with local crews and creators when a brief needs them.

Our Mumbai D2C brand has good creative but falling ROAS. What usually causes that?

In our experience with creative-strong accounts, it is rarely the ads. The common causes are checkout friction, shipping cost disclosure timing, a product page that does not answer the question the ad raised, and tracking that overstates platform-reported performance. A funnel audit usually finds more revenue than a creative refresh in this situation.

Can you produce creative in Mumbai?

Yes, through local creators and crews. Mumbai has the deepest production talent pool in India, so for shoot-heavy briefs it is usually more efficient to source there than to bring a team in. We handle briefing, direction and edit supervision.

How do we attribute store visits to digital spend?

For jewellery, luxury and high-consideration retail, the practical approach is a combination of store-visit conversion tracking in Google, unique offers or codes per campaign, and asking at point of sale. None is perfect individually; together they give a defensible directional picture, which is enough to make budget decisions.

What does a Mumbai D2C brand typically spend?

Brands doing meaningful revenue usually run ₹3L to ₹25L a month in media, plus creative production. The management fee is a small fraction of that. Below roughly ₹1.5L a month the account struggles to generate enough conversion data for the platforms to optimise properly.
