Hyderabad's economy runs on three engines: the IT and global capability centre belt around HITEC City, Gachibowli and the Financial District; the pharmaceutical and life sciences cluster around Genome Valley and the older bulk-drug corridor; and a real estate market that has expanded aggressively along the Outer Ring Road.

Two of those three come with real advertising constraints. Healthcare and pharma marketing has to work inside platform policy on health claims and Indian advertising regulation, which rules out most of the messaging that performs in unregulated categories. That is a creative problem, and it is solvable, but not by ignoring it until an account gets restricted.

What the local economy runs on

IT services and global capability centres, pharmaceuticals and life sciences, one of India's largest hospital and diagnostics sectors, real estate along the ORR corridor, and a growing D2C and food business base.

Answers

Hyderabad: frequently asked

Can hospitals and clinics advertise on Meta and Google?

Yes, within limits. Both platforms restrict health outcome claims, before-and-after imagery in several categories, and targeting based on inferred health conditions. Indian medical advertising rules add further constraints. Campaigns work when built around education, doctor credentials, facilities and convenience rather than promised results.

Does regional-language creative perform better in Hyderabad?

Frequently, yes, particularly outside the central IT corridors and in categories like healthcare, education, real estate and local services. It is worth testing Telugu against English properly rather than assuming either. English tends to hold up better in the HITEC City and Financial District belt.

What works for Hyderabad real estate lead generation?

Volume is not the problem; the ORR corridor generates plenty of enquiries. The differentiator is qualification, instant follow-up and site-visit conversion. We build the WhatsApp and CRM automation alongside the campaigns, and push site-visit and booking outcomes back to the platforms so they optimise for buyers rather than browsers.

Do you work with pharma and life sciences companies?

On the B2B side, yes: search visibility, technical content, LinkedIn presence and lead capture for export and institutional business. We do not run consumer campaigns that make therapeutic claims, which are restricted by both platform policy and Indian regulation.

Are you available for meetings in Hyderabad?

We work with Hyderabad clients remotely from Noida, over video and shared reporting. In-person visits can be arranged for onboarding or quarterly reviews when a project justifies it.
