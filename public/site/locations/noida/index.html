Noida is our home market, and it is an unusual one. Within a fifteen-kilometre radius you have Sector 62 and 63 full of IT services and software companies, Sector 18 and the mall corridor serving retail and consumer brands, the film city media cluster, and a fast-growing belt of D2C warehousing and fulfilment operations pushing into Greater Noida and the Yamuna Expressway corridor.

That mix means there is no single Noida marketing playbook. A B2B software company in Sector 62 needs LinkedIn credibility and Google Search intent capture. A D2C brand shipping from a Greater Noida warehouse needs Meta creative volume and a checkout that works on a mid-range Android phone. A dental clinic in Sector 50 needs the map pack and a phone that rings.

What the local economy runs on

IT services, software and BPO in the Sector 62–63 corridor, education and coaching, real estate across Noida Extension and the Expressway sectors, D2C fulfilment and warehousing towards Greater Noida, and a large retail and F&B base around Sector 18 and the mall belt.

Noida industries

Who we work with here, and what they need

IT services and B2B software

The Sector 62 and 63 belt is dense with technology firms selling to businesses. These need search intent capture, LinkedIn presence and long-cycle lead nurture rather than impulse-driven social ads.

D2C and ecommerce

Greater Noida and the Expressway corridor have become a fulfilment hub for consumer brands. The marketing constraint here is almost always creative volume and checkout conversion, not targeting.

Real estate

Noida Extension, Sector 150 and the Yamuna Expressway sectors generate constant high-ticket enquiry demand, with long decision cycles and a heavy dependence on follow-up speed.

Education and coaching

Competitive-exam coaching, skill training and edtech operate on sharp admission cycles, where campaigns have to peak in specific months rather than run flat.

Clinics and healthcare

Dental, dermatology, physiotherapy and diagnostics compete almost entirely in the local map pack and on how fast someone answers the phone.

Retail, F&B and lifestyle

The Sector 18 and mall corridor businesses live on local discovery, Instagram presence and offer-led footfall campaigns.

Which channels tend to work in Noida

In Noida the split is sharp: consumer businesses live on Meta plus local SEO, while the B2B and IT cluster gets far more from Google Search and LinkedIn. Getting that allocation right at the start matters more here than in a purely consumer market.

Answers

Noida: frequently asked

Where in Noida are you based?

AIOS Labs operates from Noida, Uttar Pradesh. We work with clients across Noida, Greater Noida, Delhi and the wider NCR, and remotely with brands across India and internationally. In-person meetings within NCR are straightforward to arrange.

Do you work with Noida-based B2B and IT companies?

Yes. The Sector 62 and 63 cluster is largely B2B, and that work looks different from consumer marketing: Google Search for high-intent queries, LinkedIn for credibility, long nurture sequences, and offline conversion tracking so the CRM tells you which campaigns produced actual pipeline.

Can you help a Noida clinic or local business rank in Google Maps?

Yes. For a single-location clinic or service business, Google Business Profile optimisation, citation cleanup and a genuinely useful location page typically move the map pack within four to eight weeks. It is usually the highest-return work available to a local Noida business.

What does digital marketing cost in Noida?

For a local service business, meaningful local SEO plus a small paid budget usually starts around ₹30,000 to ₹60,000 a month all-in. D2C brands running serious Meta and Google campaigns should plan for management plus a media budget of at least ₹1.5L a month for the account to generate usable data.

Do you meet clients in person?

Within NCR, yes: Noida, Greater Noida, Delhi and Gurgaon are all easy. Outside NCR we work over video, which is how most engagements run regardless of location.
