Surat is a manufacturing city on an enormous scale: man-made fabric, sarees, dress materials, and the diamond cutting and polishing industry. The business culture is trade-led: relationships, agents, wholesale volume and thin margins on huge quantities.

The interesting shift is that a growing number of Surat manufacturers are going direct to consumer, and they have a structural advantage almost nobody else has: they own the production. The barriers are consistently the same three things: product photography, catalogue and feed structure, and a website that can actually take an order from a first-time buyer who has never heard of them.

What the local economy runs on

Textile manufacturing and processing, diamond cutting and polishing, apparel and saree wholesale trade, chemicals and dyes, and a rapidly growing D2C ecommerce segment.

Answers

Surat: frequently asked

We are a Surat textile manufacturer. How do we start selling direct?

In sequence: consistent product photography, a store that can handle a large and frequently changing catalogue, clear sizing and fabric information, a returns policy a first-time buyer will accept, and only then paid acquisition. Manufacturers have a real cost advantage, but it converts into wasted spend if the storefront cannot close a stranger.

How do we manage thousands of SKUs online?

Through catalogue discipline rather than manual effort: structured product data, automated feed rules, collection logic that surfaces new designs, and merchandising rules that push what is actually selling. At Surat SKU volumes, the feed and catalogue system is the marketing infrastructure.

Is Google Shopping better than Meta for a Surat textile brand?

Usually it is the better starting point. Demand for saree, dress material and fabric categories already exists in search, and feed quality among Indian sellers is generally poor, so a well-structured feed competes effectively without a large budget. Meta then adds reach once you know which products convert.

How important is product photography?

It is the single biggest determinant of conversion in fabric and apparel. Buyers are trying to judge texture, drape and true colour from a screen. Poor photography is the most common reason a Surat manufacturer with excellent product cannot sell it online, and it is fixable.

Can you help with export enquiries?

Yes, through international search visibility, English technical and product content aimed at what overseas buyers search for, and enquiry capture with time-zone-aware follow-up. For B2B export this is largely an SEO and content exercise rather than a paid advertising one.
