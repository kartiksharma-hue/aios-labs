Privacy Policy

This policy describes what happens to your information when you visit this website or contact AIOS Labs. It is written to describe what we actually do, which is less than most privacy policies describe.

Last updated 5 September 2026

What this website collects

We ask you for nothing. This site has no contact forms, no accounts, no login, no newsletter signup and no payment flow. Every page is static, and there is nothing here for you to fill in.

The site does run the Meta Pixel. It loads on every page and records that a page was viewed. Meta receives your IP address, your browser and device details, the page you are on, and the identifiers in any Facebook or Instagram cookies your browser already carries. We use it to measure our own advertising and to build audiences on Meta. We do not upload customer lists or contact details to it, and it records no form data because the site has no forms.

  • To refuse it, block the pixel with a content blocker, use your browser's tracking protection, or adjust your Meta ad settings at facebook.com/adpreferences.
  • Meta processes this data as an independent controller under its own terms.

Third parties that receive a request

Loading any third-party file means your browser contacts that company and it sees your IP address. On this site that is: Meta, for the pixel described above; Clearbit, which serves the client brand logos shown on some pages; and Cal.com, if you open the booking page. Fonts are served from our own domain, so no font request leaves this site.

Hosting and server logs

The site is hosted on Netlify. Like any web host, Netlify records standard server logs containing IP addresses, request times, requested URLs and user-agent strings. These are used for delivering the site and for security, and they are handled under Netlify's own privacy terms.

When you contact us

If you email, call, WhatsApp, message us on Instagram or LinkedIn, or book a call through Cal.com, we receive whatever information you choose to send. We use it only to reply and to discuss working together.

Cal.com is a third-party booking service. When you book through it, Cal.com collects the name, email address and any notes you enter, and processes that under its own privacy policy.

  • We do not sell, rent or share your contact details with anyone.
  • We do not add you to a mailing list without you asking.
  • We keep enquiry correspondence for as long as it is commercially relevant, then delete it.

Client data during an engagement

If you become a client, we may be given access to your advertising accounts, analytics, CRM or store admin. That access stays inside your own accounts. We do not copy your customer data out of your systems, and we do not use it for anything other than the work you engaged us for.

Case-study screenshots published on this site have identifying client details blurred. We ask permission before publishing anything that identifies a client.

Your rights

You can ask what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email us and we will action it. In most cases the only information we hold ourselves is the correspondence you sent us: the pixel data sits in Meta's systems, not ours, so a request to delete that has to go to Meta through your Facebook or Instagram account settings.

Changes to this policy

If the site starts collecting data, for example if we add analytics or a contact form, this policy will be updated before that goes live, and the date at the top will change.
