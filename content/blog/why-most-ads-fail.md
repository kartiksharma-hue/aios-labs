The average person decides whether to keep watching in under **3 seconds**. If your ad opens slow, nothing else matters.

## Lead with the problem, not the brand

Nobody cares about your logo yet. They care about the itch you can scratch. Open on the pain.

## Show, do not tell

A visual of the result beats a sentence describing it every time.

## Three hook patterns that work

1. The blunt claim ("This fixed my sleep in a week")
2. The pattern interrupt (something unexpected on screen)
3. The callout ("If you run Meta Ads, stop doing this")
